Privacy Policy

Effective September 8, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how StalysTech handles information when you visit stalystech.com, contact us, or purchase digital products.

2. Information we collect

We may receive information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, contact-form message, purchase-related information, and communications with us. Our website and service providers may also process technical information such as IP address, browser or device information, timestamps, referring pages, and security or diagnostic logs.

3. Payment information

Checkout and payment processing are handled by Paddle or other payment providers we identify at checkout. StalysTech does not need to receive or store your full payment-card number. Payment providers process transaction, billing, tax, fraud-prevention, and compliance information under their own privacy practices.

4. How we use information

  • To process purchases and deliver digital products.
  • To respond to questions and support requests.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the website and fulfillment systems.
  • To maintain transaction, fulfillment, accounting, fraud-prevention, and compliance records.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

5. Service providers

We use service providers to operate our business, including website hosting, payment processing, cloud storage, workflow automation, and email delivery. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services. Current infrastructure may include Netlify for website hosting and forms, Paddle for checkout and payment-related services, Cloudflare for storage or infrastructure, n8n for workflow automation, and email service providers for communications and delivery.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

Our site and embedded services may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies that are necessary for site operation, checkout, security, fraud prevention, preferences, or service performance. Third-party checkout services may set their own technologies according to their policies and applicable law.

7. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including product fulfillment, customer support, security, recordkeeping, dispute resolution, and legal or tax requirements. Retention periods may vary by data type and service provider.

8. Sharing

We do not sell personal information as a business model. We may disclose information to service providers, professional advisers, authorities when legally required, or parties involved in a business reorganization, acquisition, or similar transaction, subject to applicable requirements.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or other handling of personal information. You may contact us to make a privacy request. We may need to verify your request and may retain information where legally permitted or required.

10. Security

11. Children

StalysTech products and services are intended for business users and adults and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through our commercial services.

12. International processing

Our providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, applicable safeguards are used by the relevant provider or data controller.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our services or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
