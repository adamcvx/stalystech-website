Refund Policy

Effective September 8, 2026

Digital goods are generally non-refundable

StalysTech products are digital goods that are delivered or made accessible electronically. Because a digital product cannot practically be returned after access or delivery, purchases are generally final and non-refundable once the product has been delivered, downloaded, or otherwise made accessible.

Case-by-case exceptions

We recognize that unusual circumstances can occur. StalysTech may review refund or remedy requests individually and may, at our discretion, approve a full or partial refund, replacement delivery, or other reasonable remedy. Examples that may warrant review include duplicate charges, a product that cannot be delivered because of a technical failure, a materially corrupted or unusable download that we cannot replace, or another exceptional circumstance we determine merits a refund.

Change-of-mind requests

We generally do not provide refunds for change of mind, purchasing the wrong product, failure to use the product, incompatibility that was reasonably disclosed before purchase, or dissatisfaction based solely on expectations that were not part of the product description after the digital materials have been accessed or delivered.

How to request review

Submit a request through the Contact form on stalystech.com and include the email address used for the purchase, the product purchased, the approximate purchase date, and a brief explanation of the issue. Do not send full payment-card information.

Legal rights

This policy does not exclude, restrict, or modify any refund, cancellation, or consumer rights that cannot legally be waived. Where applicable law requires a refund or other remedy, that law controls.

Payment-provider process

Approved refunds may be processed through the payment provider or merchant of record used for the original transaction. Processing and posting times can depend on that provider and the customer's financial institution.
